09:17 AM — The emergency call

A fictional fire has been reported at the flashing red point. You have a map of roads, fire stations, hospitals and nearby community facilities. You do not know live traffic, vehicle availability, hospital capacity or road accessibility.

Your goal: make an initial recommendation, receive a new control-room message, and decide whether your original plan still holds. A nearest facility is only a candidate—not automatically the best choice.

Rules: Use the map controls to turn layers on and off. Click markers to read details. Record your reasoning in the Mission Console. There may be more than one defensible answer. Never assume the map proves a road is open or a facility can accept patients.

Assessment (100): Automated map-based and objective assessment 90 marks; faculty evaluation of written reasoning 10 marks. Final grade is confirmed only after faculty enters the written marks.

The red incident and control-room alerts are invented for this classroom exercise. Parks and open spaces are not automatically designated emergency shelters.
